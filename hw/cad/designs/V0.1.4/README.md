# Equip-1 enclosure CAD — V0.1.4 (centred screen and buttons, OLED FPC, button caps, glass jig)

V0.1.4 is [V0.1.3](../V0.1.3/README.md) with the OLED active area **centred front-to-back** (1.27 mm left of
centre, user OK) for the **12 mm FPC** of the panel actually used, the button column **on the case centre
line** under **TPU button caps**, LED light tunnels, OLED locators and a printed **glass jig**. It is built from
the saved V0.1.3 file (SHA256-pinned).

**This version needs a Firehat PCB change.** Its fit checks use the *Firehat Rev 5 proposal*, a KiCad
project the user edits (`hw/cad/boards/.build/firehat-rev5/firehat-rev5-proposal.kicad_pcb`, never
regenerated by `build_boards.py`; its STEP is re-exported on every run).

**CAD packaging only.** This is not physical fit, strength, electrical, thermal or
manufacturing qualification.

- **OLED panel** modelled from the supplier drawing (wiki raw source `2026-10-30-oled-12mm-fpc-drawing`):
  cap glass, substrate, polarizer, V/A and A/A markers. Parameter groups *OLED panel* and *OLED FPC*.
- **`OledFpc`**: the 12 mm FPC as drawn, bent along two smooth curves (glass edge → down the slot → forward
  into FPC2, which is turned 180° in front of the slot), drawn for the shortest FPC (11.7 mm). The length
  check uses the tightest line + arc route. **`OledFpcContacts`**: the 4 mm, 0.3 mm stiffened contact end
  with 30 gold fingers inside FPC2.
- **OLED locators** under the ceiling: a stop ring on the bare 0.5 mm glass margin (sets the height) and 6
  posts (0.2 mm per side). Use compliant foam tape (0.3–0.5 mm) under the glass.
- **Buttons**: flat **G-Switch GT-TCA84C-H015-L10AA** (LCSC C2896628, 5.2 × 5.2 × 1.5 mm, 100 gf, 1 M cycles)
  under **printed caps in the V0.1.0 shape** (`Button1`–`Button3`, 6.6 mm square, R1.8, domed R2.0 top,
  1.5 mm above the flat top, print in TPU 95A). The caps rest 0.05 mm above the actuator; a 0.5 mm flange
  sits in a shallow channel under the skin (1.55 mm skin left). Insert the caps from inside before the board.
- **LED light block**: one Ø2.2 mm tunnel per SK6812 (0.8 mm walls, round ends), 0.65 mm skin over each LED.
- **FireWire port split** (user request): the seam steps up to the port's centre line (z 11.97). A tab in the bottom
  shell's left wall (y −0.55 … 16.2 at the split; front side flared over almost the whole wall (to y −28.5, ~9°), back ~24°, down to the seam; the lip and
  groove follow the flanks, 0.2 mm normal to each face) holds the lower half of the opening, the lid the upper half (notch + 0.15 mm), so the
  opening is a closed ring around the connector. The opening is one outline: the J2 silhouette through the wall (measured
  from the Firehat Rev 5 model) offset by 0.2 mm (`FwClearance`), instead of seven boxes and arcs instead of a lid notch open down to the seam.
- **Seam lip** (checked all round): lip 1.0 × 1.6 mm, 0.2 mm to the lid skin, 0.4 mm under the groove top (resin: fine).
  The front lip gaps (where the ROCK 2F connectors drop through the lip into their openings) stay as they were: narrowing
  them to the connector + 0.5 mm left 0.05 mm slivers between the lid's groove fillers and the port openings. The left
  gap is gone (the lip follows the FireWire tab).
- **USB-C opening**: plug outline from the Radxa model (8.94 × 3.16 mm) + 0.15 mm (was 0.25), corners R0.6 all round:
  the top corners must stay above the seam, and a seam step down to the plug centre (for a fully plug-shaped opening) does
  not fit next to the front mount inset (1.2 mm between them, the step needs 1.4). The two front blind recesses
  (`LedWin*`, labelled "LED" by inference in V0.1.0) are mount insets (user, 2026-11-02): size unchanged.
- **Cold-shoe clip mount** (`ShoeMount`, printed accessory: PETG, nylon or tough resin): a base plate under the case with two
  front fingers whose pins click into the front mount insets and a back arm whose tongue hooks into the back slot, both
  hugging the R5 bottom rolls at 0.2 mm; an ISO 518 cold-shoe foot underneath (18 × 18 × 2 mm plate on a 12 mm neck; test-fit
  on the camera). Fit: back edge first, then press the front down until the pins click in.
- **Corner spacers** (`Spacer1`, `Spacer2`, printed resin): a Ø5 tube with an unthreaded Ø2.9 bore and two fins to the
  corner walls, at the two front screws between the ROCK 2F and the Firehat (11.69 mm, from the board models). At the back
  the GPIO header couples the boards (user choice); it has about 0.6 mm play before it bottoms out, so tighten the back
  screws only snug. The walls put them on
  the screw axis; one M2.5 × 18 button-head screw per corner goes from below through the bottom boss, ROCK 2F, (front: spacer) and
  Firehat into the lid insert, and only that insert pulls the stack together (before, nothing sat between the boards).
- **Resin tolerances** (user: resin shells): seam lip-to-groove 0.4 → 0.2 mm per side (lip 0.8 → 1.0 mm thick), button cap
  rest gap 0.05 → 0.15 mm, glass-jig pocket 0.1 → 0.2 mm, counterbore Ø4.7 → Ø5.2 for M2.5 ISO 7380 button heads.
- **Heat spreader** (thermal study, user choice "inside"): a 1 mm aluminium sheet (`HeatSpreader`, laser/waterjet cut from
  the exported STEP) in a pocket in the floor, top flush with the floor (the ROCK 2F underside parts reach down to z 2.4),
  clear of the screw bosses and the USB-A leg pockets, plus a soft 14 × 14 mm gap pad (`ThermalPad`, 2 mm compressed to
  the 1.6 mm gap) from the sheet to the ROCK 2F underside below the RK3528A. Estimate: about 20 °C off the SoC
  (99 → 80 °C at an assumed 4.25 W, camera-mounted); less if the board's vias under the SoC conduct worse than modelled.
- **No vents** (camera-mounted; thermal study): side grilles, diagonal low/high grilles and corner slots were tried and
  dropped (natural venting in a 21.7 mm case carries out < 0.7 W). The floor vents were removed (no flow under
  the ROCK 2F, 1.6 mm above the floor). Thermal estimate (`scripts/thermal.py`, `.build/thermal/`): natural venting
  in a 21.7 mm case carries out < 0.7 W even with every free wall opened, so the grilles help little; air on all sides
  (camera mount) and the real power matter most.
- Mod access tried and dropped (user, 2026-11-01): side-wall knockouts, a GPIO hatch on magnets, an inside-inserted
  mod plate.
- **`GlassJig`** (printed tool, not in the device): pins in the Firehat's 4 mounting holes, pocket = glass
  + 0.1 mm, FPC opening in the front wall, relief over the FireWire shell. Assembly: FPC into FPC2, jig on,
  foam tape, glass in, press, jig off, board into the case.
- Estimates the FPC route depends on (each 0.1 mm moves the slot/FPC2 by about 0.1 mm): `Fpc2CableZ` 14.30,
  `FpcBendR` 0.5, `PanelCapThickness` 0.55.
- Tried and dropped: FPC2 behind the slot, 40/50 mm FPC panels, KSC4 / KSC2 switches (too tall for caps under
  a flat top), a raised button strip, soft pads under the caps.
