# Board models for the enclosure CAD

These are the reference bodies for the boards inside Equip-1. They are generated by
`build_boards.py` (run it with `FreeCADCmd`) into `.build/`, which is gitignored. The models
themselves are not committed: the Radxa model is a third-party download, and Firehat Rev 4 is
the board currently being ordered. Every source is SHA256-pinned, and none is modified.

| Board | Source | What the script does |
|---|---|---|
| Firehat Rev 4 | Internal KiCad 10 project (`firehat` wiki `raw/sources/2026-10-29-firehat-rev4/`, SHA256 `2e107eac…2809`) | Extracts a copy, applies the 3D fixes below, exports STEP with `kicad-cli` |
| ROCK 2F v1.0 | Radxa official PCBA model, `https://dl.radxa.com/rock2/2f/2F_pcba_20240401.zip` (SHA256 `af06e6c5…cbaa1`) | Downloads and unzips; no changes |

## Firehat Rev 4 3D fixes (copy only, KiCad source untouched)

- **VRML-only models** (40-pin header, 16-pin PCIe FPC, LED1): use their STEP twins from the `easyeda2kicad` library. STEP export can't use VRML.
- **Switches SW1–SW3** (C&K KSC4TE01J LFS): model z offset set to 1.7 mm for all three. The project had +1.5 mm on SW1 and +1.0 mm on SW2/SW3, which sank the bodies 0.2–0.7 mm into the board. The C&K KSC4 TE datasheet gives 5.2 ± 0.2 mm from the PCB to the actuator top.
- **BT2** (LOTES AAA-BAT-046-K01, CR1220 holder): it had no model. The script fetches the EasyEDA model (LCSC C2926854) and places it with offset (−8.74, −7.09, −8.99). That puts the two locating pegs (8.00 mm apart, per LOTES drawing AP-AAA-BAT-046) on the footprint origin and the holder on the board underside. The model is 4.49 mm deep against 3.70 mm in the drawing, so it's conservative.
- **Still without models:** eight 0402 passives (0.5 mm tall; not relevant for fit).

## Findings for the enclosure

- **Earlier stand-in was wrong:** the old stand-in ROCK 2F (`equip-1-beta-backup/pcb/rock-2f`) used a 57.85 × 48.9 mm hole pattern and a 1.6 mm board. The reference bottom shell copied that pattern, so its screw holes are 0.15 / 0.08 mm off the real board. The top-shell towers (58 × 49 mm, the HAT pattern) are right.
- **Trial fit in V0.1.0** (ROCK 2F on the bottom bosses, Firehat on the top towers):
  - The 40-pin header engages 4.4 mm.
  - The new switches overlap the V0.1.0 button caps by 34–36 mm³ each.
  - The connectors overlap the port openings by 1–5 mm³ (0.1–0.2 mm).
  - The Firehat corners touch the top shell's inner roll.

## Limits

These are mechanical reference bodies only. Radxa's model is v1.0 (2024-04-01), and the Firehat
model is only as good as its component models. Neither has been measured against a physical board.
